Four ways to draw the ring

Each ring opens at the cursor when you press the Sense Panel (the haptic pad under your thumb). These use your 8 Options+ ring slots; the Options+ Folder slot can't be carried over, so it shows as an empty slot. Move the pointer over a ring to see the selection follow the direction, as it will with the mouse. Move back to the centre to cancel.

All four: drawn by the tray (Direct2D, layered window, no WinUI start-up), so the ring appears instantly. Slender's graphite and copper colours. A haptic tick fires when the selection changes slot. Release the Sense Panel or click to run the slot. Esc or the centre cancels. Each app profile can have its own 8 slots; empty slots fall back to All apps.
