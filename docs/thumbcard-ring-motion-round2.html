One card for the whole thumb wheel

Clicking the thumb wheel on the mouse picture opens this card. It now holds everything the thumb wheel does in the app you picked: the normal turn and its speed, and holding the top button while turning, with its own speed. Shown for Microsoft Edge with your real settings (Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab). The sliders, toggles and tabs respond to clicks; the action buttons only look like they open the picker.

B. Four ways the ring opens and closes

Your Bezel ring with example slots. Press Open and Close, or tick Slow motion to watch each one at a quarter speed. In Slender the ring takes your choice from the first frame: you never wait for the animation to finish.

Both parts: Slender's graphite and copper. Card sizes are real flyout sizes. The ring is drawn by the tray (Direct2D), so motion is a short burst of redraws, about 15 frames, then nothing.
Reduced motion: when Windows "Animation effects" is off, every ring style just fades in over 90 ms.
Reply with a card number and a motion number, a mix ("card 3 with 4's steppers, motion 2"), or changes.
